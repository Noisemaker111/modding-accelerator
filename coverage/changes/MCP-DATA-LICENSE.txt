The step files in this folder whose "names" is "mcp" (the pairs touching a release before
1.14.4) are derived from MCP mapping data: the SRG tables and name CSVs published on
Forge's Maven as de.oceanlabs.mcp:mcp, de.oceanlabs.mcp:mcp_config and
de.oceanlabs.mcp:mcp_stable. Each such file names its exact source artifacts in its
"source" field. The mapping files themselves are downloaded at generation time and are
never committed or shipped; what is committed is the derived difference between two
releases. Only the identifier and name columns of the CSVs are read, never the javadoc.
